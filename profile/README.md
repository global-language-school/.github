# 🌍 Global Language School

**Global Language School** is an AI-powered educational ecosystem providing end-to-end language training, academic support, and relocation guidance for students worldwide. 

We bridge the gap between online technology and personal mentorship to ensure a seamless transition into international education and life abroad.

## 🚀 What We Do

* **AI-Powered Language Learning:** Personalized study paths, interactive practice, and adaptive smart tools for rapid language acquisition.
* **Exam & Interview Preparation:** Automated mock tests, state certification prep, and AI-driven visa/consul interview simulations.
* **Admissions & Relocation Support:** Step-by-step guidance for university applications, documentation, and adapting to a new country.
* **Hybrid Mentorship:** Combining our digital platform with dedicated offline and human support at every stage of your journey.
